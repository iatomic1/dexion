# Price Alerts

## Overview
Price Alerts let you automatically track token changes on Dexion.
You define a condition, and Dexion notifies you when it happens.

## Metrics Available
Each alert can monitor one of the following metrics:

- **Price** - Triggered when the token price crosses your target.
- **Liquidity** - Alerts when liquidity increases or decreases past your threshold.
- **Market Cap** - Monitors changes in the token’s total market capitalization.
- **Holders** - Triggers when the number of token holders changes beyond your condition.

---

## Delivery Channels
You can choose where Dexion delivers your alerts:

- **In-App (Web)** – Displays directly in your dashboard.
- **Telegram** – Requires linking your Telegram account in <Link href="https://dexion.pro/settings" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Settings</Link>
- **Email** – Sent if you signed up with email or linked Google.
- **Webhook** – For developers who want to handle alerts programmatically.

---

## Alert States
- **Active** – Currently monitoring and can trigger anytime.
- **Paused** – Temporarily stopped.
- **Completed** – Triggered and closed (for non-repeatable alerts).

---

## Example
Example alert setup:
> Notify me on Telegram when `$STX` price goes above 2.00.

Once this condition is met, Dexion instantly sends a Telegram message with the details.
