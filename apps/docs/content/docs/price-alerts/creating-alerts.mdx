---
title: Creating Alerts
---

1. Open the <Link href="https://dexion.pro/alerts" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">**Alerts**</Link> page in your Dexion dashboard.
2. Click **Create Alert**.
3. Select a token or paste its **contract address**.
4. Choose a **metric** (price, liquidity, market cap, or holders).
5. Define your **operator** and **target value** (e.g. Price > 0.5).
6. Pick one or more **delivery channels**.
7. Decide whether the alert should be **repeatable**.
8. Click **Create Alert**.
