# Getting Started

## Sign Up

To access Dexion features like Price Alerts, Wallet Management, and Pulse, you need a Dexion account.
You can sign up using any of the following options:

### **1. Google Sign-In**
Sign up instantly using your Google account.
- Click **Continue with Google** on the sign-in page.
- Grant permission to access your email and basic profile.
- You’ll be redirected to your Dexion dashboard automatically.

### **2. Email Sign-Up**
Use your email address to create an account.
- Click **Sign up with Email**.
- Enter your name, email, and password.
- Verify your email through the link sent to your inbox.
- Once verified, you can log in securely.

<Image
  src="/signup-email.png"
  alt="Email Sign-Up"
  width={800}
  height={450}
/>

---

### **3. Wallet Sign-In (Stacks)**
If you already have a Stacks wallet, you can sign in directly without a password.
- Click **Connect Wallet**.
- Approve the connection in your wallet extension.
- Your Dexion account will automatically link to that wallet address.

  src="/signup-wallet.png"
